# Choose products on Ozon

Use the ChatGPT Work cloud browser to research product models on the web, then inspect Ozon's visible catalog and product pages. Keep Ozon browsing in the same browser session so the user can complete a verification challenge there if one appears. Do not assume a CAPTCHA will appear.

First get the product type, delivery city, intended use, must-have properties, preferences, and a budget ceiling if the user has one. Ask for missing essentials in one short message. Keep preferences separate from requirements. If the user has no budget, proceed without a price limit.

Before searching Ozon, research strong models across the wider web using recent, relevant comparisons, independent reviews, and product roundups when available. Check the publication date, source, and fit to the user's requirements. Treat rankings and promotional lists as leads, not proof of quality. Build a shortlist of identifiable models to look for on Ozon, noting the links that informed it.

Search Ozon for those models and inspect up to ten relevant product cards. If none of the shortlisted models are available, look for suitable Ozon alternatives and explain that they differ from the researched shortlist. Verify the delivery city shown on the site. If Ozon shows a CAPTCHA or other human check, pause and ask the user to take over **the Work cloud browser**, complete it on the site, and return control. Wait for that handoff before continuing from the same session. If access remains blocked, explain the limitation and stop. Never ask the user to send the challenge answer, cookies, login, or payment details in chat.

For each candidate, read the exact model or variant, direct product URL, visible specifications, rating, review count, and prices from its card. Treat a search-list price as preliminary. Record prices with their payment or account conditions separately. Call an item within budget only when a price without an extra condition is confirmed; otherwise label the budget result as conditional or unverified. Check the city again if the card displays a different delivery location.

For up to three promising candidates, inspect customer-written reviews on Ozon. If there are no readable Ozon reviews for the exact model or variant, look for customer reviews of that exact model or variant on other marketplaces. For each source, provide its link, the number of reviews actually read, and whether they refer to the exact variant or only a combined listing. Keep ratings, review counts, and findings from different marketplaces separate. Summarize concrete repeated positives and negatives from the inspected sample; do not transfer claims from another model or variant, treat customer opinions as verified specifications, or claim that a review's authenticity was established. If no matching reviews are available, say so.

Recommend at most five products. Include the ordinary price and any conditional price used, relevant confirmed features, rating and review count with their source and scope, a brief review finding with source and sample size, direct Ozon link, delivery city, and time checked. Link the outside research that informed the shortlist and any external marketplace reviews used. Flag every important property or price you could not verify. Do not add products to a cart, place an order, or submit personal information.
